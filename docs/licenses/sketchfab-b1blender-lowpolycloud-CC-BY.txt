Used as internal/assets/models/storm-cloud.glb (internal/assets.GanonStormCloud),
the dark storm clouds that gather above the arena at the start of the
ganon-battle field's final-form defeat cutscene (cmd/ganon-battle,
internal/renderer/ganon.go's GanonBattleStormCloudObjects). Tinted a dark
blue-grey (unlike its original bright blue look) to read as an ominous
storm rather than a fair-weather sky.

7 simple flat-color low-poly cloud meshes in one GLB (no embedded texture),
loaded with gltf.ParseParts like the Ganon boss assets; a handful of the 7
are picked and placed by hand above the arena.
