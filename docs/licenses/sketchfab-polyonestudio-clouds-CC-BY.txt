Used as internal/assets/models/temple-cloud.glb (internal/assets.TempleCloud),
floating clouds added to the temple field's sky (cmd/game /
internal/renderer/demo.go's templeCloudObjects). Grassland's own clouds
(internal/assets.CloudTexture, a flat billboard) are unaffected.

16 solid block-style cloud meshes in one GLB (no skin, no embedded texture —
color comes from a flat material baseColorFactor), loaded with
gltf.ParseParts like the Ganon boss assets; only a handful of the 16 are
used, picked and placed by hand above/around the temple building.
