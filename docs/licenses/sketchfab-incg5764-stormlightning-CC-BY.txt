Used as internal/assets/models/ganon-lightning.glb (internal/assets.GanonLightning),
the lightning bolt struck down onto the boss during the ganon-battle field's
final-form defeat cutscene (cmd/ganon-battle, internal/renderer/ganon.go's
GanonBattleLightningObject).

3 low-poly forked lightning bolt meshes in one GLB (simple blue-white
emissive material, no embedded texture), loaded with gltf.ParseParts like
the Ganon boss assets; only one of the 3 is used, picked for having the
most compact/vertical shape.
