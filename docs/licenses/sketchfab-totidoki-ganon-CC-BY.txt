This asset is used at the explicit request of the project owner, made

publicly or commercially, this asset should be reconsidered.

Two files derived from this same Sketchfab model are used:

- internal/assets/models/ganon-boss.glb (humanoid Ganondorf form) —
  Sketchfab's "converted" glTF export, used for the throne-room field
  (cmd/ganon-hall).
- internal/assets/models/ganon-boss-battle.glb (green, horned beast
  form) — converted from the author's original FBX, with all embedded
  textures downscaled to <=1024px and re-encoded as JPEG (originally
  ~66MB of uncompressed PNGs, some 4096px) to keep the compiled game a
  reasonable size; only baseColorTexture is used by this project's
  renderer, so normal/metallic-roughness/emissive textures were
  compressed too even though they're not read. Used for the battle-scene
  field (cmd/ganon-battle).
