Used as internal/assets/models/rock-debris.glb, for the falling-rock/rubble
collapse effect in the throne-room field (cmd/ganon-hall) and the matching
"wipe" rock used for the page transition into the battle-scene field
(cmd/ganon-battle).

The original download (GLB, 1k textures, ~11.6MB) contains 28 rock/prop
meshes across several categories (Big/Mid/Small stones, runic carved
stones, and two small decorative props). Only the 19 Big/Mid/Small stone
meshes are used here; the runic/decorative meshes were dropped as unused.
The 3 baseColorTexture images actually referenced by the kept meshes were
downscaled from 1024px to 512px and re-encoded as JPEG (quality ~80) to
reduce the embedded size (this engine only reads baseColorTexture, so the
other unused texture slots in the original file were dropped entirely
rather than compressed).
